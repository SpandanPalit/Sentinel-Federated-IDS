# Sentinel demo data

Sentinel uses locally downloaded traffic records from **N-BaIoT** by Yair Meidan, Michael Bohadana, Yael Mathov, Yisroel Mirsky, Dominik Breitenbacher, Asaf Shabtai and Yuval Elovici (2018). The authors made the dataset available through the UCI Machine Learning Repository under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Source and citation](https://doi.org/10.24432/C5RC8J)

The [data guide](../docs/data.md) describes how to reproduce the demo on your computer.
